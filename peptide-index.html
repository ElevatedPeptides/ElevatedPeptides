Metabolic & GLP-1 Research

Dual Agonist

Tirzepatide

Dual GLP-1 & GIP receptor agonist

Tirzepatide is a synthetic dual agonist that activates both GLP-1 and GIP receptors — two incretin hormone pathways involved in glucose regulation and appetite signaling. Research models have studied dual-receptor activation for its effects on insulin secretion, gastric emptying, and energy balance, making it one of the most actively researched compounds in metabolic and obesity-related literature. Approximate elimination half-life referenced in pharmacokinetic literature: ~5 days, which underlies the once-weekly dosing interval studied in trials.

Typical Range2.5 mg – 15 mg per dose
TitrationStart 2.5 mg; increase in 2.5 mg steps every 4 weeks as studied, up to a 15 mg ceiling referenced in trials

Triple Agonist

Retatrutide

GLP-1 · GIP · Glucagon receptor agonist

Retatrutide is a next-generation triple agonist engineered to activate GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors simultaneously. This triple-pathway approach is studied for its potential to influence energy expenditure alongside appetite and glucose regulation, positioning it at the frontier of metabolic peptide research. Pharmacokinetic literature references an elimination half-life of roughly 6 days, consistent with once-weekly research dosing intervals.
